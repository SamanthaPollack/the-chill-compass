Heroes Sail Free 🇺🇸

A heartfelt thank-you from Margaritaville at Sea to the real-life heroes who serve our communities and country every day.

Image: Margaritaville at Sea

🎖️ Claim Your Heroes Offer ↗
Verify with GOVX through Margaritaville at Sea

Created as a heartfelt thank-you, the Heroes Sail Free cruise program honors the brave individuals who serve our communities and country every day. From U.S. active and former duty military and first responders to educators, medical personnel, and government employees, this program is Margaritaville at Sea's way of giving back to the real-life heroes who deserve to relax, recharge, and escape to an island state of mind.

Since launching with GOVX in 2022, Margaritaville at Sea has welcomed over 100,000 U.S. military, first responders, teachers, and everyday heroes on board for well-deserved getaways filled with sunshine, sea breezes, and unforgettable memories.

🎖️ Who's eligible

🪖Military
active & former
🚒First responders
🚓Police
🩺Healthcare
🍎Educators

Government employees may qualify too. Eligibility is confirmed through a free GOVX account.

🛳️ How it works

  1. Verify with GOVX through Margaritaville at Sea. Heroes create a free GOVX account and get verified before booking.
    🎖️ Claim Your Heroes Offer ↗
  2. Book the Heroes Sail Free cabin online. The offer is a $0 cruise fare for the first two guests in an Interior stateroom on Islander and Paradise, on select sailings, while space lasts.
  3. Pay only the extras. Taxes, fees and port expenses are still due at booking. Guests 3 and 4 in the room pay regular fares.
  4. Want a different date or room? Verified heroes can get an extra 20% off other sailings and stateroom types, subject to availability.

Offer details come from Margaritaville at Sea and can change at any time. Free cabins are limited, and blackout dates may apply.

💛 Bring your crew along!

Sailing as a hero? We would love to help your friends and family join you on the same sailing, whether that's a cabin next door, a family reunion or a whole group of shipmates. Our travel advisors will find the best available rates and handle the details, at no extra cost.
